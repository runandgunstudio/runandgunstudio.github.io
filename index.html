Sticky Situation

Playable Prototype Android APK 2D Platformer Landscape
Download Test APK Watch Gameplay

Sticky Situation is a 2D physics platformer where you launch a sticky little blob across walls, ceilings, and tight gaps. Dodge spiked traps, reach each checkpoint flag, and keep moving forward. It takes seconds to learn, with just enough challenge to keep you trying one more time.

Sticky Movement

Jump, stick to walls and ceilings, and time your launches to get past tricky gaps and tall climbs.

Fast Checkpoints

Hit flag checkpoints along the map and tap once to restart instantly whenever you miss a jump.

Playable Skins

Fun character skins designed to unlock as you reach higher checkpoints and progress further.
