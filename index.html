First Project

Sticky Situation

Prototype Ready Android & iOS 2D Rage Platformer Landscape
Watch Gameplay Request Test Build

Sticky Situation is a funny, rage-inducing 2D platformer where you fling a sticky little blob past cruel jumps, tight walls, and spiked traps. One tiny slip sends you right back down—making every move hilarious, frustrating, and impossible to walk away from. The playable prototype is ready now, with just a few finishing touches left on the maps and extras to be wrapped up very soon.

Normal Mode: Drag & Sling

Pull back, aim, and sling your blob onto walls and ceilings. Simple to pick up in seconds, but brutal to master under pressure.

Hard Mode: Tap Timing

Want a real test of patience? Switch to tap-based timing jumps where every split second matters and mistakes punish you fast.

Funny Fails & Quick Retries

Built for laugh-out-loud wipeouts, shareable rage moments, and fast checkpoint retries so players always go for one more try.
